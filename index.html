About Us

At My Friends Salon And Spa, we redefine the art of relaxation. Located in the heart of Chennai, our spa brings together centuries-old wellness traditions with cutting-edge technology. Each guest is welcomed into an oasis of elegance—where personalized therapies, bespoke pampering, and a calming atmosphere converge to create the ultimate luxury experience.
Our expert therapists combine authentic techniques with premium products to deliver unforgettable massages, facials, and body treatments. Whether seeking relief, radiance, or relaxation, every moment at our spa is designed to rejuvenate both body and mind.
